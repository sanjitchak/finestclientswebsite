1. Background & Program Objective

Mohsin Raza operates an established digital-services company from Varanasi with more than a decade of market experience and a team supporting digital services and Salesforce Marketing Cloud. The company currently generates approximately ₹3,00,000 to ₹4,00,000 in monthly revenue, with client acquisition relying mainly on referrals.

The initial service focus will be social media and performance marketing. Finest Clients and Mohsin will use the first month to validate the offer, positioning, sales process, and Meta campaigns in India, focusing initially on one or two proven segments such as hospitality and medical-related businesses. Once the first service and market approach are proven, the acquisition engine will expand internationally, with Salesforce Marketing Cloud considered only after the initial offer is validated.

The objective of this Program is to install an end-to-end Full Done-For-You (DFY) Client Acquisition & High-Ticket Sales Engine, beginning in India and then expanding to the United States, United Kingdom, Singapore, UAE, and Australia, generating qualified appointments and scaling the company to 20 cumulative paying clients within 180 days.

2. Nature of Engagement: Done For You

Except for foundational offer positioning and messaging work in Week 1 requiring Mohsin's direct collaboration, Finest Clients will build, manage, and optimize the client acquisition systems from start to end. This includes funnel development, technical setup, tracking, Meta ad creative production, campaign management, lead qualification, appointment routing, nurture automation, and cold-email systems. Finest Clients will create the Meta ad creatives. Media buying and systems will be actively managed by Success Manager Sanjit, while co-founder Mousam provides offer and sales strategy, objection handling, call reviews, and closing guidance. Mohsin and his team will conduct the sales calls and close prospective clients. From Month 2, Finest Clients will also provide LinkedIn and YouTube organic growth strategy, with implementation, shooting, and editing handled by Mohsin's team.

3. Program Deliverables

  • Offer, Niche & Positioning Architecture: Audit and refine Mohsin's social media and performance-marketing offer, pricing, messaging, and positioning. Initial research will focus on one or two proven segments, including hospitality and medical-related businesses, before broader scaling.
  • Full DFY Meta Advertising Engine: Complete Meta campaign build, audience targeting, copy, creative production, pixel tracking, and ongoing media buying managed by Success Manager Sanjit on the Client's ad account. Finest Clients will create the Meta ad creatives.
  • High-Converting Funnel & Booking Infrastructure: Test and deploy the suitable funnel structure, which may include opt-in forms or a VSL, together with lead qualification, calendar scheduling, automated booking reminders, and nurture sequences.
  • Cold Email & Automated Follow-Up Systems: Set up and manage cold-email outreach, personalized audit/video outreach where applicable, AI-assisted nurture automation, and follow-up systems designed to improve appointment show-up rates. Any third-party email, outreach, AI, automation, CRM, or related software costs remain separate and are payable by the Client.
  • LinkedIn & YouTube Organic Growth Strategy: From Month 2, provide strategy and direction for LinkedIn and YouTube to build credibility and attract warm inbound leads. Mohsin's team will handle implementation, shooting, editing, content production, and posting.
  • High-Ticket Sales Frameworks & Coaching: Offer refinement, sales-call reviews, objection-handling guidance, closing frameworks, and ongoing support from Mousam, Sanjit, and the Finest Clients team.
  • Dedicated 180-Day Team Support: Direct communication and ongoing acquisition-system support throughout the six-month engagement.

4. Phased Implementation Roadmap

Weeks 1 to 2 • Offer Audit, Positioning & Campaign Build

Collaborate with Mohsin to audit the current offer, pricing, positioning, sales process, and competitor activity. Select the initial service and niche focus, then build funnels, qualification, tracking, Meta ad creatives, cold-email systems, and nurture automations. Prepare the campaign for launch by the end of Week 2.

Weeks 3 to 4 • Phase 1: Go-Live & Appointment Generation

Launch and test Meta campaigns for the Indian market using a Month 1 ad budget of ₹15,000–₹25,000. Optimize the offer, lead quality, appointment show-up rate, and sales process, with a working target of at least two paying Indian clients during the first month.

Month 2 • Phase 2: International Expansion & Organic Inbound

After the India offer is validated, expand Meta acquisition to the United States, United Kingdom, Singapore, UAE, and Australia, with an initial international Meta budget discussed at ₹50,000–₹80,000 per month. Begin LinkedIn and YouTube organic growth strategy while refining sales SOPs and call handling.

Months 3 to 6 • Phase 3: Scale & Sales Optimization

Continue optimizing the proven acquisition channels, review sales calls and objections, improve the closing process, and scale campaign investment in line with results and the Client's delivery capacity while working toward the cumulative 20-paying-client milestone.
