1. Background & Program Objective

Evangelin operates an established marketing and technology firm (registered Private Limited in early 2024, domain active since August 2023) headquartered in Chennai, with a past operational and referral network across Mumbai / Navi Mumbai. The firm specializes in delivering combined marketing, automation, and tech solutions tailored specifically for factory owners and industrial manufacturers (generating ₹5+ Crore annual turnover), helping them establish global positioning and secure direct export inquiries.

With an established track record of servicing over 70 manufacturers and delivering bundled high-ticket offerings at ₹1,50,000 to ₹2,50,000 retainers, the company currently generates ₹5,00,000 to ₹7,00,000 in monthly revenue. Having expanded delivery and channel partner infrastructure, Evangelin is prepared to scale to ₹20,00,000 to ₹30,00,000 per month. Current client acquisition relies on outbound cold calling by two dedicated sales reps in Chennai and organic referrals from Mumbai, with initial Meta advertising trials underway.

The objective of this Program is to install an end-to-end Full Done-For-You (DFY) Client Acquisition & High-Ticket Sales Engine targeting factory owners and industrial manufacturers across Pan-India, generating consistent high-ticket appointments (~40 show-ups/month), and scaling the firm to 20+ cumulative high-ticket clients.

2. Nature of Engagement: Done For You

Except for foundational offer positioning and messaging audit in Week 1 requiring direct collaboration with Evangelin (approximately 30 minutes daily), Finest Clients will build, manage, and optimize the client acquisition systems from start to end. This includes funnel development, technical setup, tracking, Meta ad creative production, campaign management, lead qualification, appointment routing, and email lead-generation systems if required. For video-based Meta ad creatives, Finest Clients will provide the scripts and produce the creatives; Evangelin will only be responsible for recording the requested video footage from those scripts. Media buying and technical tools will be actively managed by Success Manager (Sanjit), while co-founder Mousam provides weekly 1-on-1 strategy sessions, sales closing frameworks, call reviews, and sales rep training with Amudha, Evangelin, and the sales team. Evangelin and her team conduct the sales calls and close prospective clients. YouTube and Instagram organic growth support is strategy-only and does not include shooting, content production, posting, or done-for-you account management.

3. Program Deliverables

  • Industrial Niche Offer & Positioning Architecture: Audit and refine Evangelin's bundled marketing and technology offering (automation, global positioning, export inquiry generation) at ₹1,50,000 to ₹2,50,000+ retainers tailored specifically for factory owners and manufacturers.
  • Full DFY Meta Advertising Engine: Complete Facebook & Instagram campaign build, industrial audience targeting, copy, creative production, pixel tracking, and ongoing media buying managed directly by Success Manager (Sanjit) on your business ad account. Finest Clients will prepare the video scripts and finished ad creatives; Evangelin will only need to record and supply the requested video footage based on those scripts.
  • High-Converting Funnel & Booking Infrastructure: Complete funnel design (opt-in/forms/VSL), custom intake questionnaire to qualify factory owners by turnover (₹5 Cr+) and budget, calendar scheduling integration, and automated booking reminders.
  • WhatsApp, Email Follow-up & Email Lead-Generation Systems: Deployment of Finest Clients' internal tools and AI automations for instant lead follow-up, show-up reminder sequences (targeting ~50% show-up rate), and automated nurturing. If additional leads are required, Finest Clients will also handle the setup and management of email systems for lead generation. Any required WhatsApp AI automation, outreach, email, or related third-party software tools are additional operating costs payable separately by the Client.
  • YouTube & Instagram Organic Growth Strategy: Finest Clients will provide organic growth strategies and a targeted content roadmap to establish authority in the manufacturing niche and attract warm inbound leads. This is a strategy-only deliverable and excludes shooting, editing, content creation, posting, and done-for-you execution or account management.
  • High-Ticket Sales Frameworks & Weekly Coaching: Weekly Saturday 1-on-1 sales strategy and call-review sessions with co-founder Mousam, objection handling battlecards, closing scripts, and sales team training for Amudha, Evangelin, and internal sales reps.
  • Dedicated 60-Day Team Support: Priority direct WhatsApp communication channel with Success Manager Sanjit and the Finest Clients team throughout the 2-month engagement.
