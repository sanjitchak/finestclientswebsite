Program Objective

The Program is built for creative agencies and marketing agencies that want to escape referrals, low ticket clients, price negotiation, and random content dependence. The goal is to help you install predictable appointment and high ticket sales systems so you can attract clients who value strategy, judgment, outcomes, and long term partnership.

Priya's Initial Client Acquisition Plan

  • Begin in the Indian market with one niche, one ideal client profile, one positioning, and one offer.
  • The initial client offer will be priced at approximately ₹1,50,000 and focused on social media, ad creation and creatives, and performance marketing.
  • Use the initial Indian-market phase to prove the offer and positioning and work toward the first 4 to 5 clients before scaling the proven system internationally.
  • Start with Facebook and Instagram campaigns, a simple VSL funnel, back-end qualification, reminders, follow up, and sales optimization. Webinars, workshops, Google Ads, and YouTube are not part of the initial cash-flow phase.
