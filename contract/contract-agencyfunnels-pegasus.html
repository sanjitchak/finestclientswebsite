Program Objective

The Program is designed to build and optimize a single-channel Meta Ads system for Pegasus solution, targeting US commercial insurance agency owners and principals. The system is phone-first, with no email nurture or email outreach included. Meta retargeting, phone, compliant SMS, and LinkedIn follow-up will support the campaign as described below.

Program Deliverables

  • Pre-launch audit and setup guidance for Meta Business Manager, business verification, the ad account, the Facebook Page, Meta Pixel installation on pguss.com, tracking, booking calendar, and lead alerts.
  • Setup, management, and optimization of the agreed Meta Ads campaigns.
  • Thank-you-screen setup directing each lead to the hosted benchmark PDF and booking calendar immediately after submission.
  • One Meta Ads campaign and one ad set for the initial campaign structure, using Facebook and Instagram Feeds and Stories and excluding Audience Network.
  • Launch strategy for four creatives: founder talking head, two static concepts, and one workflow carousel, together with hooks, campaign copy guidance, and creative refresh recommendations.
  • From month 3, subject to sufficient audience data, a prospecting and nurture structure using form submitters and other eligible warm audiences for retargeting.
  • Weekly tracking and optimization guidance for cost per qualified lead, qualified rate, time to first call, connect rate, booked calls, pilots, campaign frequency, and creative performance.
  • Phone-first follow-up guidance covering calls, compliant SMS, LinkedIn touches, lead logging, and retargeting. Email marketing, email nurture, and email outreach are excluded.

Implementation Roadmap

Pre-launch

Complete business verification, account and pixel setup, the DOI audience, lead magnet, Meta Ads setup, booking path, four launch creatives, lead alerts, calling stack, and end-to-end mobile testing before spend begins.

Months 1 to 2

Run one Meta Ads campaign and one ad set against the DOI Custom Audience, review lead quality and call outcomes, and refresh or replace underperforming creative using real campaign data.

From Month 3

Subject to usable audience size and delivery, allocate approximately ₹22,000 to prospecting and ₹8,000 to nurture retargeting, with distinct creative for each audience.

Optimization

Review the agreed weekly and monthly metrics, refresh the audience list and creative, identify lost-lead patterns, and pause or rebuild parts of the system when the documented hard-stop criteria are reached.
