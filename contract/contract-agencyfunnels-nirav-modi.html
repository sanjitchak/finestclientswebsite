Program Objective

The Program is designed to help your software development and AI services company build a predictable client acquisition channel beyond Upwork and referrals. The initial focus is on the international market and on selecting one clear niche, ideal client profile, problem, positioning, and offer before campaigns are launched.

The engagement combines Done-With-You consulting with additional execution support from our team. The goal is to strengthen your agency positioning, build an appointment system, and support you in converting qualified opportunities into recurring high ticket software-services clients.

Program Deliverables

  • Access to the Agency Funnels DWY systems, trainings, templates, resources, and implementation assets.
  • Research into relevant competitors and up to three candidate positioning directions to help finalize one niche, ideal client profile, problem, and offer.
  • Offer, messaging, positioning, and pricing support so your company is presented as a specialized agency rather than a replaceable freelancer or general software vendor.
  • Collaborative setup and execution support for the initial client acquisition system using the mutually selected channels, which may include cold email, LinkedIn direct outreach, and paid ads.
  • VSL funnel, form, calendar, campaign, tracking, automation, lead qualification, and follow up support as required by the selected channel plan.
  • High ticket sales training, sales-call review, pitch and objection-handling support, and guidance on follow up and closing.
  • One-to-one guidance, accountability, campaign review, troubleshooting, and team support during the Program.
  • Content was discussed as a longer-term inbound channel, but ongoing content creation is not included in the agreed execution scope unless both parties add it in writing.

Implementation Roadmap

Week 1

Audit the business and current portfolio, research competitors and positioning options, and finalize one niche, ideal client profile, problem, offer, messaging, and channel plan.

Week 2

Build and configure the selected campaign, tools, funnel, qualification flow, tracking, automation, calendar, and follow up systems.

Week 3

Launch the initial campaign and begin measuring delivery, replies, lead quality, booked calls, and other available performance data.

Weeks 4 to 6

Work toward stable initial appointments while refining the offer, messaging, qualification, sales calls, and follow up using real data.

Month 2 to 3

Continue testing and optimization toward the first paying client. Cold email and other outbound channels may require up to three months of testing before client results.

Day 90 to 180

Scale the proven channel, improve conversion and closing performance, and work toward the Program's client target.
