01, Diagnosis

The business already has proof. The pricing does not reflect it.

The issue is not whether Digital Edge 360 can deliver. The issue is whether the market is being asked to evaluate the agency as a premium specialist or as another local service provider.

What Digital Edge 360 has

  • Six partner certifications across Google, Meta, Shopify, Adobe, ISO, and MSME.
  • Ducati, TruBoy BBQ, Nayantara, JetChoice, Das Capital, Hotel Jiva, and Edify among named clients.
  • 150+ Shopify builds delivered and Shopify Plus certification.
  • Self-described as amongst the top 3% marketing agencies in India.
  • 100+ active clients with full in-house SEO, PPC, SMM, content, and marketplace teams.

What it currently earns

  • Typical Kolkata client pays ₹30,000 per month for PPC, SEO, and marketplace combined.
  • Those same clients run up to ₹5 lakhs per month in ad spend through the agency.
  • Current digital marketing retainer revenue sits around ₹2.5 to ₹3 lakhs per month.
  • Net profit on the marketing arm is approximately ₹1 lakh per month.
  • Client acquisition is referral-led, local, and not yet expanded outside the existing market.
This is a positioning gap, not a capability gap.

An agency holding six partner certifications, with Ducati as a client, 150+ Shopify builds, and a top 3% positioning claim, is being compensated at ₹30,000 to manage ₹5 lakhs of ad spend. The credentials are elite. The pricing is commodity. The fix is not simply more leads. It is a corrected offer, then a predictable pipeline to reach buyers who pay on value rather than proximity.

03, Acquisition channel

The channel is a diagnosis, not a default.

Most agencies sell one channel because it is the only one they operate. We select the channel in Week 1, after the positioning work is complete, based on where the repositioned offer will convert fastest.

A

Paid Ads

Meta and Google. Fastest route to first appointment, typically within 48 hours of launch. Strongest where the offer converts on a short consideration cycle.

B

Cold Email

Hyper-personalised at scale. Dynamic video and voice personalisation rather than templated sequences. Requires approximately four weeks of domain warmup before results compound.

C

Cold DM

LinkedIn and direct outreach. Highest intent per contact, lowest volume. Strongest for enterprise and international contract values.

Digital Edge 360 already operates LinkedIn Sales Navigator and has run cold email campaigns. Those assets are not discarded. They are repositioned behind a corrected offer, which is what determines whether any channel converts.

Untargeted volume on any channel is spam. The offer is what turns it into pipeline.

06, Compounding layer

Paid acquisition buys time. Content compounds it.

This section separates a campaign from an asset. The first 180 days should not only produce clients. It should create the market signal needed to build a long-term acquisition engine.

1

Stage 1, Months 1 to 3

Acquisition

The selected channel produces predictable appointments. Cost per client is known and repeatable. Revenue becomes forecastable rather than referral-dependent.

2

Stage 2, Months 3 to 6

Multi-channel

A second channel is layered in. Paid, cold email, and cold DM operate together rather than in isolation. Pipeline stops depending on any single platform, algorithm, or inbox provider.

3

Stage 3, Months 6+

Content and Organic Funnels

YouTube, LinkedIn, and long-form content built on the corrected positioning. This is the compounding asset. Content published once continues generating appointments for years without incremental spend.

Our own channel has not published a new video in over a year and continues to generate approximately 100,000 views monthly, along with inbound appointments. That is the difference between renting attention and owning it.

Paid channels stop the moment payment stops. Content does not. The purpose of the first 180 days is not only to acquire 20 clients. It is to fund and inform the content engine that acquires clients after the engagement ends. The positioning work in Week 1 is what makes that content convert, which is why it comes first.

Warm inbound leads close at materially higher rates than cold traffic, because trust is established before the first conversation.
