Program Objective

The Program is designed to help you build a predictable client acquisition system for your regulation-driven ESG consulting and related SaaS offering. The initial focus is on targeted listed companies and the relevant sustainability, finance, recruitment, and board-level decision makers.

The engagement combines Done-With-You outreach systems and consulting with Done-For-You content support. The goal is to strengthen your positioning in the cold market, create awareness, generate qualified appointments, and support a high ticket sales process for long-term enterprise clients.

This Agreement covers the ESG consulting and related SaaS offering discussed during the sales call. Green finance is a separate offer with a separate ideal client profile, positioning, and scope, and is not included in this Program unless added in writing by both parties.

Program Deliverables

  • Access to the Agency Funnels DWY systems, trainings, templates, and implementation assets.
  • Offer, positioning, ideal client profile, messaging, and pricing support for the ESG consulting and related SaaS offering.
  • Consulting and implementation support to establish and optimize the third-party outreach tools purchased in your name.
  • Setup and guidance for targeted cold email and LinkedIn outreach, including campaign structure, automation, tracking, lead research, and follow up.
  • Done-For-You content creation and support for market awareness and appointment generation across LinkedIn content, email content, SEO, and blog content.
  • Research support to identify current decision makers within the agreed target companies using your database and publicly available sources.
  • Transparent access to campaign activity and performance data, including available delivery, open, reply, appointment, and lead-quality metrics.
  • High ticket sales guidance, campaign review, troubleshooting, and strategy support through calls, learning platform access, and team guidance during the Program.

Implementation Roadmap

Week 1

Finalize the ESG offer, ideal client profile, positioning, messaging, and initial email and LinkedIn content. This phase requires approximately 30 minutes of your time each working day with our team.

Week 2

Set up the campaigns, outreach tools, automation, tracking, domains, and supporting systems in the accounts purchased and controlled by you.

Week 3

Take the initial email and LinkedIn campaigns live, begin measuring performance, and refine the setup based on early data.

Weeks 4 to 5

Work toward initial appointments, review lead quality and responses, and optimize the messages, channels, follow ups, and sales process.

Day 30 to 180

Continue outreach, publish and refine LinkedIn, email, SEO, and blog content, and scale or adjust what is supported by campaign and sales data.
