Program Objective

This engagement covers Offer 1 only: your ₹1,00,000 per month performance marketing and brand growth retainer for D2C brands. The Program is built for performance marketing agencies that want to escape referrals, low ticket clients, price negotiation, and random content dependence. The goal is to help you install a predictable, paid-ads-driven appointment system and a high ticket sales process so you can attract D2C founders who value strategy, judgment, outcomes, and long term partnership. The Program supports a fully faceless brand approach; a personal face on camera is not required.

Nature of Engagement: Done For You

This is a Done For You engagement. We do everything on the acquisition system: messaging, positioning, offer structuring, VSL, funnel, campaigns, tracking, automations, and follow up systems are created, set up, and managed by us. The only areas where we guide rather than execute are sales and the appointment setter: we train and advise you on sales calls, closing, and setter hiring and management, but we do not take sales calls or close clients on your behalf. All accounts, funnels, tools, and assets are owned by you, and your execution on sales directly affects results.

  • A dedicated one-to-one account manager while the engagement is active.
  • Regular coaching, review, troubleshooting, and strategy calls, plus access to the Finest Clients community.
  • Offer, messaging, storytelling, and positioning built by us for your agency, including a faceless brand angle if required.
  • Your VSL funnel and appointment system built and set up by us: VSL, landing and booking pages, Meta ads campaigns, tracking (Meta Pixel), automations, reminders, and follow up systems.

Implementation Roadmap

Week 1

We finalize your messaging, storytelling, positioning, faceless brand angle, and offer, and audit your current assets and technical setup. Requires roughly 30 to 60 minutes of your input per session.

Week 2

We build the system: VSL, funnel, landing and booking pages, campaigns, tracking, automations, and follow up sequences.

Weeks 3 to 4

Ads go live. First appointments typically start within 2 to 3 days of launch. We review lead quality, qualification, and show up rates, and refine messaging and campaigns.

Month 2 Onward

We optimize campaigns, appointments, and follow ups using real data, and guide you on sales calls, objections, and closing ratio. Budget and systems scale as cash flow allows, including a commission-only setter.
