1. Background & Program Objective

Manpreet Kaur has operated an agency since 2019, initially providing Android application and website development and serving global clients through Upwork, Fiverr, and direct engagements, including clients in Singapore, Australia, and Canada. After a two-year medical break and changes in the programming market, the agency expanded into personal branding, AI automations, agentic AI, AI video, and content distribution. Manpreet has an existing delivery network that includes editors, remote team members, and personal-branding partners.

The agency currently has two clients and has generally attracted engagements in the ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 range. Manpreet intends to move away from low-ticket work and sell personal-branding and AI-related services beginning at approximately ₹1,50,000, with a target range of ₹1,50,000 to ₹5,00,000 per client. The long-term stated revenue goal is ₹1 Crore per month.

The objective of this Program is to install an end-to-end Full Done-For-You (DFY) Client Acquisition & High-Ticket Sales Engine, select and validate a niche based on Manpreet's existing proof and partner results, generate qualified appointments, and help the agency onboard 20 high-ticket clients paying at least US$1,500 through YouTube, email, Meta, Facebook, LinkedIn, and related automation systems.

2. Nature of Engagement: Done For You

Except for foundational offer positioning in Week 1, which requires direct collaboration with Manpreet for approximately 30 minutes per day, Finest Clients will build, manage, and optimize the client acquisition systems from start to end. This includes offer and positioning, funnel development, technical setup, tracking, Meta appointment campaigns when used, email outreach, Facebook and LinkedIn automations, WhatsApp and AI automations, lead follow-up, appointment routing, and sales-system support. Manpreet will attend and conduct the sales calls. Success Manager Sanjit will handle backend systems and appointment performance, while co-founder Mousam will support offer creation, positioning, sales training, and weekly founder Q&A or strategy calls. Direct support will be available through WhatsApp or Slack throughout the 180-day engagement.

3. Program Deliverables

  • High-Ticket Offer & Positioning Architecture: Audit and refine Manpreet's personal-branding, AI automation, AI video, and content-distribution services into a no-brainer high-ticket offer beginning at US$1,500 / approximately ₹1,50,000, with the initial niche selected from the areas discussed on the call based on available results and proof.
  • Full DFY Meta Appointment Engine: When Meta is used, build and manage the Facebook and Instagram appointment campaigns, funnel, audience targeting, tracking, follow-up, and booking journey rather than routing low-intent traffic directly to WhatsApp.
  • High-Converting Funnel & Booking Infrastructure: Build the appropriate opt-in, form, VSL, or other funnel selected for the offer, together with qualification, calendar scheduling, appointment routing, and automated reminders designed to improve show-up rates.
  • Email Lead-Generation System: Set up and manage outbound email as an additional acquisition engine. Manpreet will arrange three outreach domains / email properties required for the system.
  • Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp & AI Automations: Deploy the applicable outreach and follow-up automations. Facebook and LinkedIn can be used to generate the first clients when Manpreet does not initially want to fund Meta ads. Manpreet will create three to five Instagram accounts where Instagram outreach is used.
  • YouTube Funnel & Organic Growth Strategy: Develop YouTube authority-building, funnel, content, and distribution strategy for Manpreet's chosen niche. This engine may begin in Month 1 rather than waiting until Month 3.
  • High-Ticket Sales Frameworks & Weekly Coaching: Weekly access to co-founder Mousam for offer work, sales training, objection handling, and review of recorded sales calls, with the objective of improving Manpreet's closing ratio.
  • Dedicated 180-Day Team Support: Direct WhatsApp or Slack communication with Success Manager Sanjit and the Finest Clients team throughout the six-month engagement.

4. Phased Implementation Roadmap

Week 1 • Offer, Positioning & Pricing

Work with Manpreet for approximately 30 minutes per day to finalize the niche, high-ticket offer, positioning, messaging, and pricing. The agency will not position below US$1,500 for the high-ticket offer discussed on the call.

Weeks 2 to 3 • Systems, Funnel & Campaign Launch

Set up outbound email, the selected funnel, booking and reminder automations, and the applicable Meta, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, and AI systems. Target campaign readiness by Week 3 and initial booked appointments within approximately 48 hours after paid campaigns go live.

Week 4 • High-Ticket Sales Optimization

Review Manpreet's recorded calls, improve objection handling and closing, and work toward closing one to two clients in the first month. The working acquisition model discussed is approximately 80 monthly appointments, improved show-up rates through automation, and a minimum 10% closing ratio.

Months 2 to 6 • Scale Three Acquisition Engines

Scale the validated system into higher-value markets, including the US, UK, and Singapore, using the three main engines discussed: YouTube, email, and Meta. Continue Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, and AI automation support as applicable, refine sales performance, and work toward 20 onboarded high-ticket clients within 180 days.
