1. Background & Program Objective

Go Chiro Pro operates as a specialized agency providing AI SEO, SEO, and PPC services, currently generating approximately ₹5,00,000 per month with revenue dependence on referrals and Upwork systems. The objective of this Program is to eliminate inconsistent lead flow and scale Go Chiro Pro to ₹15,00,000 to ₹20,00,000 per month.

Finest Clients will provide a complete Full Done-For-You (DFY) client acquisition infrastructure—including offer repositioning (transitioning current ₹30,000–₹50,000 packages into high-ticket $1,000–$1,500 offers), Meta ads setup, funnels, outbound email systems, and appointment setting—so that your sole operational focus is taking qualified sales calls and closing deals.

2. Scope of Done-For-You Deliverables

  • High-Ticket Offer Restructuring: Reposition and package Go Chiro Pro's AI SEO, SEO, and PPC services from ₹30,000–₹50,000 offers to premium high-ticket $1,000–$1,500 offers (approx. ₹85,000–₹1,25,000+) targeted at high-intent decision makers.
  • Full DFY Meta Advertising Engine: End-to-end setup, copywriting, creative strategy, technical pixel/conversion tracking, and ongoing campaign management for Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram).
  • High-Converting VSL & Funnel Setup: Direct guidance, funnel structure, landing page optimization, lead capture forms, automated reminders, and calendar booking integration.
  • Cold Email & Outbound Systems: Setup and deployment of automated B2B email marketing and outbound lead generation infrastructure as needed to diversify appointment volume.
  • Done-For-You Appointment Pipeline: Automated qualified appointment generation routed directly onto your calendar, pre-framing prospects for sales calls.
  • High-Ticket Closing Frameworks: Complete sales battlecards, objection handling scripts, and closing frameworks specifically customized to close $1,000–$1,500 SEO & PPC deals without high-pressure tactics.
  • Dedicated Strategic Support & Account Management: Ongoing weekly performance optimization, tracking review, troubleshooting, and direct support throughout the 180-day engagement.
