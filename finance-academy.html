The enrolment gap

A strong finance programme can still lose good applicants between the ad and the admissions call.

A finance professional will not enrol only because a page lists more modules. The funnel must make the outcome relevant to their role, build trust and guide the next step clearly.

  • Broad finance messaging can attract clicks from the wrong roles, markets or price points.
  • Senior professionals need credible proof, faculty trust and a clear career or commercial use case.
  • Qualified registrants can be lost when reminders, admissions calls and payment follow-up rely on memory.
We do not promise more students. We build a clear path that educates, qualifies and follows up with each applicant.
