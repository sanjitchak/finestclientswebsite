Program Objective

The Program is built for marketing agency owners, consultants, and B2B SaaS founders who are below ₹20 lakh per month in revenue and stuck because lead generation is unpredictable, referral dependent, and inconsistent. The goal is to install one client acquisition system that produces predictable qualified appointments and high ticket sales, so you stop competing on price and start attracting clients who value strategy, judgment, outcomes, and long term partnership.

The measurable outcome we work toward is 20 paying, onboarded high ticket clients in 180 days. Not 20 leads and not 20 appointments. Acquisition starts in the India market because testing costs are lower, and once the funnel is proven it is scaled to international markets such as USA, UK, Singapore, Dubai, and Australia.

Program Deliverables

Phase 1 - Offer Engineering and Positioning Lock (Week 1 to 2)

  • One to one Offer Engineering call to rebuild your positioning, niche, ideal client profile, and pricing structure so you stop competing on price.
  • Master Offer Sheet covering your new positioning, ICP, pricing tiers, guarantee structure, and competitive differentiation.
  • Sales page copy written for your new offer, ready to deploy on your website, sales decks, and outreach.

Phase 2 - VSL Funnel Build and India First Launch (Week 2 to 4)

  • Complete VSL funnel build including landing page, video script, calendar integration, and retargeting pixels, built by our team.
  • 3 video ad scripts written from Meta Ads Library competitor research in your niche.
  • Meta ad account setup including pixel, dataset, business manager, payment, and audience layers.
  • India campaign launch conducted together with our team on live calls.

Phase 3 - AI Qualification, Creative Engine and Sales Training (Week 4 onwards)

  • AI lead qualification bot setup using ManyChat and WhatsApp Business with qualification flows, slot confirmations, and 24 hour and 1 hour reminders to reduce no shows.
  • AI creative engine access with pre built workflows and template prompts so fresh ad variations can be produced weekly without new filming.
  • Weekly sales call reviews, with one call per week personally reviewed, working toward moving your close rate from around 10% to around 25%.
  • International market expansion playbook to scale the proven VSL from India to USA, UK, Singapore, and Dubai.

Implementation Roadmap

Week 1 to 2

Offer engineering and positioning lock. Fix niche, ideal client profile, offer, pricing, and messaging, and produce the Master Offer Sheet and sales page copy.

Week 2 to 4

Build the VSL funnel, landing page, ad scripts, tracking, and Meta ad account, then launch the first India campaign together on live calls.

Week 4 to 8

Install AI lead qualification and the creative engine, begin weekly sales call reviews, and optimize appointments, show up rate, objections, and follow ups using real campaign and call data.

Day 60 to 180

Scale what is working, add international markets once India converts, install the referral engine from month 3, layer in cold email and cold DM, and keep pushing close rate.
